Nabby vs yt-dlp-webui

Different languages, different scope, same engine.

The short version

yt-dlp-webui (marcopeocchi) is a mature Go-based frontend with job management, dashboards, and configuration. Nabby is a minimal Python/Flask paste-and-grab UI with no persistent state. Both wrap yt-dlp; the resemblance stops there.

Choose yt-dlp-webui if…

  • You want visible job management: queues, retry buttons, download history.
  • You prefer a Go binary over a Python stack for your self-hosted services.
  • You want built-in basic authentication for a protected instance.
  • You're setting up a shared instance for a small group of trusted users.

Choose Nabby if…

  • You want the smallest possible footprint — one Flask app, one systemd service, done.
  • You deliberately don't want a dashboard, history, or jobs page cluttering the surface.
  • You want no files kept on disk after download for privacy or disk-space reasons.
  • You prefer vanilla HTML/CSS/JS over a React SPA.

Honest notes

yt-dlp-webui is a more complete sysadmin tool. Nabby is closer to a web-form bookmarklet. If you want to leave the tab open and queue up ten downloads overnight, use yt-dlp-webui. If you want to grab one video from a text message your friend sent and move on, Nabby is faster to use.
